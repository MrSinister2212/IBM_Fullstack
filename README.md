#Simple Interest Calculator
A simple calculator that computes Simple Interest using the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and time period.
Formula
Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100
Where:
- Principal (P): Initial amount of money
- Rate (R): Annual rate of interest in percent
- Time (T): Time period in years
Example
For:
- Principal = 1000
- Rate = 5%
- Time = 2 years
The simple interest is:
(1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100
Usage
Run the simple interest calculator and enter the principal, rate, and time values. The program calculates and displays the simple interest.
