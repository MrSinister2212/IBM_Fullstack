# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, welcoming, and inclusive experience for everyone.

We are committed to creating a community where all contributors are treated with dignity and respect.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and considerate toward others
- Providing constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes
- Being open to different ideas and perspectives
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory or offensive comments
- Intimidation or bullying
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional environment

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and whenever an individual is representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated fairly and appropriately.

## Enforcement Guidelines

### 1. Correction

Minor inappropriate behavior may result in a private warning and an explanation of why the behavior was unacceptable.

### 2. Warning

Repeated or more serious violations may result in a formal warning and temporary restrictions on participation.

### 3. Temporary Ban

Serious violations may result in temporary suspension from project interaction.

### 4. Permanent Ban

Repeated or severe violations may result in permanent removal from the project community.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is based on the Contributor Covenant and has been adapted for this project.
